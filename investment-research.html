Comprehensive Equity Research Solutions for Professional Investors

Sophisticated equity research platforms have evolved significantly beyond basic data providers, now offering integrated research ecosystems that combine deep fundamental data, advanced screening capabilities, and proprietary analytics. This analysis evaluates the leading solutions for professional equity investors across different investment styles and firm sizes.

Our review compares top platforms including FactSet Research Systems, S&P Capital IQ Pro, Bloomberg Terminal, Refinitiv Workspace, and Morningstar Direct. The assessment examines research depth, analytical tools, data quality, model integration, and overall workflow effectiveness for equity analysis professionals.

Key Findings

  • FactSet offers the most comprehensive research solution for equity professionals, with superior company models, screening tools, and ownership data
  • S&P Capital IQ Pro provides exceptional private company coverage and transaction data at a more moderate price point
  • Morningstar Direct delivers the strongest fund analysis capabilities alongside solid equity research tools
  • Bloomberg remains essential for real-time market data but falls behind specialized platforms for in-depth equity research functionality
