Economic Research

Professional resources for macroeconomic analysis, forecasting, and policy monitoring

Economic research platforms provide essential data, analytics, and insights into macroeconomic trends, policy developments, and global economic conditions. This category examines specialized resources that enable financial professionals to monitor economic indicators, analyze monetary policy, and incorporate macroeconomic perspectives into investment and business decisions.

Our evaluations focus on data quality, analytical tools, forecasting capabilities, and content depth. We assess each platform's effectiveness for different professional roles including economists, strategists, portfolio managers, and business planners seeking reliable economic intelligence.

Professional Economic Research and Forecasting Solutions

Comprehensive economic intelligence platforms integrate data, analytics, and expert insights to support sophisticated macroeconomic analysis and forecasting. This review evaluates leading solutions that provide the breadth and depth of economic content required by professional economists and financial analysts.

Our analysis compares Moody's Analytics, Oxford Economics, IHS Markit (S&P Global), The Economist Intelligence Unit, and FocusEconomics. The assessment examines data coverage, forecasting methodologies, analytical tools, and integration capabilities for different professional applications.

Key Findings

  • Moody's Analytics provides the most comprehensive platform with exceptional forecasting models and scenario analysis capabilities
  • Oxford Economics offers superior global coverage with the strongest country-level models and industry-specific forecasts
  • The Economist Intelligence Unit delivers the strongest policy analysis and geopolitical risk integration
  • Pricing varies significantly based on user count, data breadth, and model access, with total costs scaling substantially for enterprise-wide deployments
