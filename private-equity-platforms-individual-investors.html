Best Private Equity Platforms for Individual Investors in 2026

Comprehensive analysis and comparison of platforms democratizing access to private equity investments

Overview of Private Equity Platforms for Individual Investors

The private equity landscape has undergone a significant transformation in recent years, with innovative platforms breaking down traditional barriers that once restricted access to institutional investors and ultra-high-net-worth individuals. Today, a new generation of investment platforms is democratizing access to private company investments, growth equity, and buyout opportunities for qualified individual investors at much lower minimums than conventional private equity funds.

These platforms leverage technology, regulatory innovations like the JOBS Act, and creative fund structures to provide access to an asset class that has historically delivered premium returns compared to public markets. From direct investments in individual private companies to diversified fund-of-funds approaches, these platforms offer various entry points into private markets with different risk profiles, investment minimums, and liquidity characteristics.

In this comprehensive guide, we evaluate the most effective private equity platforms across three categories:

  • Direct Private Company Investment Platforms: Marketplaces offering access to individual private company deals and opportunities
  • Private Equity Fund Access Platforms: Services providing access to institutional private equity funds and managers
  • Diversified Private Market Solutions: Platforms offering diversified exposure to private equity and related alternatives

Our evaluation methodology examines each platform's access quality, investment minimums, fee structures, due diligence processes, track record, liquidity provisions, and overall value proposition. We've analyzed these platforms in detail to provide insights into which solutions best serve different investor profiles and objectives within the private equity landscape.

Direct Private Company Investment Platforms

AngelList

9.5/10

Overview: AngelList has evolved from a startup community into the premier platform for accredited investors to access curated private company deals led by experienced operators and investors. Through its AngelList Venture platform, investors can participate in individual deals, thematic "Rolling Funds," and managed portfolios across stages from pre-seed to growth equity.

Key Features:

  • Access to syndicates led by top angel investors and operators
  • Rolling Funds offering quarterly subscription to thematic strategies
  • Deal-by-deal investment options with transparent terms
  • Managed fund offerings with professional portfolio construction
  • Comprehensive legal and administrative infrastructure
  • Detailed deal memos and investment theses
  • Secondary market opportunities through AngelList Stack

Pricing:

  • No platform fees for investors
  • Carry fees typically 15-20% on profits (varies by syndicate/fund)
  • Management fees 0-2% depending on investment vehicle
  • Minimum investments: $1,000-$5,000 for individual deals
  • Rolling Fund minimums typically $10,000-$50,000 quarterly

Pros:

  • Exceptional access to high-quality deal flow
  • Transparent economics and investment structures
  • Relatively low minimums for private startup investing
  • Strong track record with multiple unicorn investments
  • Comprehensive infrastructure handling legal complexity

Cons:

  • Limited to accredited investors only
  • Extreme illiquidity with 7-10+ year investment horizons
  • Highly variable deal quality across syndicates

Ideal For:

Accredited investors seeking direct exposure to venture capital and startup investments with relatively low minimums. Particularly valuable for those who want to build a diversified portfolio of early-stage investments led by experienced operators with sector expertise and skin in the game.

EquityZen

9.3/10

Overview: EquityZen specializes in providing access to shares in late-stage private companies through its secondary market platform. The service enables accredited investors to purchase private shares from existing shareholders (often employees or early investors) in established pre-IPO companies, offering exposure to mature private companies with clearer paths to liquidity.

Key Features:

  • Access to shares in established late-stage private companies
  • Both single-company investments and thematic fund products
  • Detailed company research and valuation analyses
  • Secondary market providing liquidity to existing shareholders
  • Company profiles with growth metrics and competitive positioning
  • Transparent transaction process and documentation
  • Self-directed and managed portfolio options

Pricing:

  • Transaction fees of 5% on investment amount
  • No ongoing management fees for single-company investments
  • Fund products carry 1.5% management fee and 10% carried interest
  • Minimum investments typically $10,000-$50,000 per opportunity
  • Fund minimums starting at $20,000

Pros:

  • Access to established companies with clearer risk profiles
  • Typically shorter time to potential liquidity than early-stage
  • Detailed research materials and company information
  • Transparent pricing relative to latest funding rounds
  • Streamlined transaction process and documentation

Cons:

  • Limited to accredited investors only
  • Potential for illiquidity if companies delay going public
  • Higher minimums than some early-stage platforms

Ideal For:

Accredited investors seeking exposure to established late-stage private companies with shorter expected holding periods. Particularly valuable for those who want to access pre-IPO opportunities in well-known companies without the extreme uncertainty of early-stage startup investing.

Republic

9.0/10

Overview: Republic has pioneered inclusive private investing through its hybrid platform offering both Regulation Crowdfunding opportunities open to all investors and Regulation D offerings for accredited investors. The platform spans startups, real estate, gaming, and crypto projects with an emphasis on curated deals and educational content for investors new to private markets.

Key Features:

  • Regulation Crowdfunding deals accessible to non-accredited investors
  • Accredited-only deals with higher investment capacity
  • Multiple asset classes including startups, real estate, and crypto
  • Curated deal flow with detailed due diligence
  • Note structure simplifying legal complexity for investors
  • Educational content for private market investing
  • Republic Core fund products offering diversified exposure

Pricing:

  • 2% transaction fee on investment amount
  • Carried interest of 6% on investment profits
  • Non-accredited minimum investments as low as $100
  • Accredited-only deals typically $1,000+ minimum
  • Fund products starting at $10,000

Pros:

  • Accessible to non-accredited investors
  • Extremely low minimum investments
  • Curated deals with transparency on selection criteria
  • Diversification across multiple private asset classes
  • Strong educational resources for new private investors

Cons:

  • Regulation Crowdfunding deals have investment limits
  • Generally early-stage deals with high risk profiles
  • Limited secondary trading opportunities

Ideal For:

Both accredited and non-accredited investors seeking access to early-stage private investments with low minimums. Particularly valuable for those new to private investing who want to gain experience across multiple deals while limiting individual position sizes.

Private Equity Fund Access Platforms

iCapital Network

9.6/10

Overview: iCapital Network provides access to institutional-quality private equity funds, hedge funds, and other alternative investments through its technology-enabled platform. The service partners with top-tier asset managers to create feeder fund structures that lower minimums while preserving access to premier private equity fund managers that typically require multi-million dollar commitments.

Key Features:

  • Access to premier institutional private equity fund managers
  • Comprehensive due diligence materials and manager research
  • Simplified subscription and administration processes
  • Consolidated reporting across alternative investments
  • Educational resources on private equity strategies
  • Secondary marketplace for limited liquidity options
  • White-glove investor relations support

Pricing:

  • Platform access through financial advisors or direct
  • Administration fee typically 0.40-0.75% annually
  • Underlying fund fees: 1.5-2% management + 20% carried interest
  • Minimum investments typically $100,000-$250,000 per fund
  • Some multi-manager products available at lower minimums

Pros:

  • Access to institutional-quality private equity managers
  • Reduced minimums compared to direct fund investments
  • Comprehensive due diligence materials and support
  • Streamlined subscription and administration
  • Technology platform simplifying complex investments

Cons:

  • Still relatively high minimums for individual investors
  • Additional layer of fees beyond underlying funds
  • Long-term illiquidity with 10+ year commitments

Ideal For:

High-net-worth accredited investors seeking access to institutional-quality private equity funds with lower minimums than direct investments. Particularly valuable for sophisticated investors who want access to blue-chip private equity managers without meeting their typical multi-million dollar minimums.

Moonfare

9.4/10

Overview: Moonfare provides streamlined access to top-tier private equity funds through a digital platform focused on user experience and educational content. The service emphasizes careful fund selection and portfolio construction, offering both individual fund investments and curated portfolio solutions to qualified investors.

Key Features:

  • Curated selection of top-tier private equity and venture funds
  • Proprietary due diligence methodology and selection process
  • Digital onboarding and subscription processes
  • Portfolio builder tools with allocation recommendations
  • Moonfare Secondaries Market for liquidity options
  • Comprehensive educational materials and insights
  • Performance tracking and portfolio analytics

Pricing:

  • One-time fee of 0.5-1.0% on committed capital
  • Annual administration fee of 0.3-0.5%
  • Underlying fund fees (varies by fund): 1.5-2.5% management + 20% carried interest
  • Minimum investments starting at €50,000-€100,000 per fund
  • Portfolio solutions available from €250,000

Pros:

  • Strong focus on user experience and simplicity
  • Rigorous fund selection and due diligence process
  • Secondary market offering partial liquidity options
  • Transparent fee structure and reporting
  • Excellent educational content for private markets

Cons:

  • European focus with more limited US offerings
  • Higher overall fee load with platform + fund fees
  • Still significant minimums for many individual investors

Ideal For:

Sophisticated private investors seeking a user-friendly platform for accessing institutional-quality private equity with moderate minimums. Particularly valuable for those new to private equity investing who appreciate the educational resources and guided approach to portfolio construction.

CAIS

9.2/10

Overview: CAIS provides financial advisors and their clients with access to a curated menu of alternative investments including private equity funds, hedge funds, and private credit. The platform emphasizes advisor education and due diligence support through its CAIS IQ learning system and partnership with Mercer for independent fund research.

Key Features:

  • Advisor-focused platform for alternative investments
  • Independent due diligence from Mercer
  • CAIS IQ learning system for advisor education
  • Streamlined subscription and reporting processes
  • Access to private equity, credit, real estate and hedge funds
  • Integrated platform for allocation and monitoring
  • White-glove service model for advisors

Pricing:

  • Platform fees negotiated by financial advisor firms
  • No direct fees for end investors beyond advisor fees
  • Underlying fund fees: 1.5-2.5% management + 20% carried interest (typical)
  • Minimum investments typically $100,000-$250,000 per fund
  • Feeder funds may have higher or lower minimums based on terms

Pros:

  • Independent due diligence from respected research provider
  • Strong educational resources for advisors
  • Comprehensive alternative investment options
  • Streamlined operational and reporting infrastructure
  • Integrated with major custodians and advisor systems

Cons:

  • Only accessible through financial advisors
  • Less direct control for end investors
  • Fee structure depends on advisor arrangements

Ideal For:

Investors working with financial advisors who want access to institutional alternative investments with professional guidance. Particularly valuable for those who prefer an advisor-managed approach to private market investing rather than self-directed platforms.

Diversified Private Market Solutions

9.3/10

Overview: Yieldstreet offers individual investors access to a diverse range of income-generating alternative investments including private equity, private credit, real estate, art finance, and structured products. The platform specializes in creating investment vehicles that provide exposure to private market opportunities with targeted income distributions and defined liquidity timelines.

Key Features:

  • Multi-asset alternative investment platform
  • Income-focused private market opportunities
  • Prism Fund offering diversified alternatives exposure
  • Individual deal investments across multiple categories
  • Digital subscription and investor reporting
  • Regular income distributions from many investments
  • Mix of short, medium and long-duration opportunities

Pricing:

  • Management fees typically 1-1.5% annually
  • Performance fees 0-20% depending on investment structure
  • Minimums from $2,500 for Prism Fund
  • Individual opportunities typically $10,000-$15,000 minimum
  • Some offerings limited to accredited investors

Pros:

  • Strong focus on income-generating alternatives
  • Relatively low minimums for alternatives exposure
  • Diverse asset classes beyond traditional private equity
  • Some options available to non-accredited investors
  • Defined investment horizons with liquidity targets

Cons:

  • Less exposure to growth-focused private equity
  • Some asset classes have limited performance history
  • Quality and risk varies significantly across offerings

Ideal For:

Investors seeking income-focused alternative investments across multiple private market asset classes. Particularly valuable for those who prioritize regular distributions and defined liquidity timelines over pure growth equity exposure in their private market allocation.

9.1/10

The platform emphasizes long-term portfolio solutions with extremely low minimums, making alternatives accessible to nearly any investor regardless of accreditation status.

Key Features:

  • Diversified private market portfolio strategies
  • Core real estate investment foundation
  • Expansion into venture capital and private credit
  • Open to both accredited and non-accredited investors
  • Automatic investment and portfolio management
  • Quarterly liquidity windows (with limitations)
  • Detailed performance reporting and asset updates

Pricing:

  • Management fee approximately 1% annually
  • Potential performance fees on certain investments
  • Minimum investment of just $10
  • Premium account levels from $1,000-$100,000
  • No additional fees for account administration

Pros:

  • Exceptionally low minimums for private market access

  • Automatic diversification across investments
  • Transparent performance reporting and asset updates
  • Limited quarterly liquidity options

Cons:

  • Limited to Fundrise's proprietary investments
  • Less choice and customization than other platforms
  • Liquidity restrictions during market stress periods

Ideal For:

Beginning alternative investors seeking broad private market exposure with minimal investment amounts. Particularly valuable for those who want a professionally-managed portfolio of private assets without the high minimums or accreditation requirements of traditional private equity.

Specialized Recommendations

For First-Time Private Equity Investors

Best Choice: Fundrise

Investors new to private markets will find Fundrise's approach exceptionally accessible, with minimums as low as $10 and no accreditation requirements. The platform's automated portfolio construction and diversification across multiple private asset classes provides immediate exposure to alternatives without the complexity of selecting individual deals or funds. For those taking their first steps into private market investing, Fundrise offers a simplified on-ramp with transparent reporting and limited liquidity provisions that other platforms can't match for newcomers.

For Sophisticated Individual Investors

Best Choice: iCapital Network

High-net-worth individuals with sophisticated investment knowledge will benefit from iCapital's access to institutional-quality private equity funds. The platform's ability to provide entrée to top-tier managers with reduced minimums (typically $100,000-$250,000 versus multi-million direct commitments) makes it ideal for experienced investors who want exposure to the same private equity funds utilized by institutions and family offices. The comprehensive due diligence materials and streamlined subscription process further enhance its value for sophisticated private investors.

For Direct Startup Investors

Best Choice: AngelList

Accredited investors specifically interested in venture capital and startup investing will find AngelList's extensive ecosystem unmatched for deal quality and access. The platform's combination of syndicates led by experienced operators, thematic Rolling Funds, and managed venture portfolios provides flexible options for building a diversified early-stage investment strategy. For those willing to accept the extreme illiquidity and binary outcomes of startup investing, AngelList offers the most robust infrastructure for building a venture portfolio with relatively modest minimum investments.

For Income-Focused Alternative Investors

Best Choice: Yieldstreet

Investors seeking income-generating private market investments will appreciate Yieldstreet's focus on alternative yield opportunities across multiple asset classes. The platform's structured approach to creating defined-term investments with regular distribution schedules provides an attractive complement to traditional fixed income portfolios. For those who prioritize current income over long-term capital appreciation in their alternatives allocation, Yieldstreet's diverse offerings across real estate, legal finance, art, and private credit provide unique yield-focused opportunities not readily available elsewhere.

For Pre-IPO Investors

Best Choice: EquityZen

Investors specifically targeting late-stage private companies approaching public markets will find EquityZen's secondary market platform ideally suited to their needs. The platform's focus on established companies with clearer paths to liquidity provides a distinctly different risk-return profile than early-stage venture investments. For those seeking to access well-known private companies before they go public, EquityZen offers transparent pricing, detailed company research, and a streamlined process for acquiring pre-IPO shares with investment horizons typically measured in months or a few years rather than the decade-long commitments of traditional private equity.

For Diversified Private Equity with Liquidity

Best Choice: Hamilton Lane Private Assets Fund

Investors seeking institutional-quality private equity exposure with some liquidity provisions will find the Hamilton Lane Private Assets Fund offers an optimal balance. The fund's unique evergreen structure with quarterly redemption windows provides access to diversified private markets with greater liquidity than traditional closed-end funds. For those who want exposure to a professionally-managed portfolio spanning direct investments, secondaries, and primary funds across stages and strategies, Hamilton Lane's $50,000 minimum and simplified tax reporting create an attractive entry point to institutional private markets investing.

Expert Perspectives

"The democratization of private equity represents one of the most significant shifts in asset management over the past decade. Innovative platforms are creating access points that bypass traditional gatekeepers and minimum investment thresholds, allowing individual investors to diversify beyond public markets. However, this accessibility creates new responsibilities for investors to understand the inherent illiquidity, fee structures, and risk profiles that differentiate private markets from traditional investments."
— Andrea Auerbach, Global Head of Private Investments, Cambridge Associates
"As private markets platforms proliferate, the critical differentiator becomes the quality of deal access and due diligence rigor. Many platforms can offer private investment structures, but few can consistently source high-quality opportunities and conduct institutional-grade due diligence at scale. Investors should prioritize platforms with demonstrated expertise in manager selection or direct investment underwriting, as the dispersion of returns in private markets far exceeds what we observe in public markets."
— Josh Lerner, Professor of Investment Banking, Harvard Business School
"The innovation in private equity access platforms is creating valuable new options for investors, but also introduces complexity in comparing different approaches. The traditional closed-end fund structure with 10+ year commitments sits alongside evergreen vehicles, semi-liquid structures, and direct investment opportunities. Each structure involves different trade-offs between liquidity, diversification, and potential returns that investors must carefully evaluate against their specific financial objectives and constraints."
— Sandra Robertson, Former CEO and CIO, Oxford University Endowment

Our Evaluation Methodology

Our comprehensive assessment of private equity platforms for individual investors is based on a rigorous methodology that examines multiple dimensions of access quality, investment characteristics, and user experience. Each platform receives a score based on the following criteria:

Access Quality and Deal Flow (25%)

We evaluate the quality, uniqueness, and consistency of investment opportunities available through each platform. This includes assessing the track record of fund managers or individual companies, competitive positioning, and historical performance when available. We place particular emphasis on whether platforms provide access to opportunities that would otherwise be unavailable to individual investors.

Fee Structure and Economics (20%)

The economics of private equity investing significantly impact returns, especially when compounded over long holding periods. We assess management fees, carried interest, transaction fees, and any platform-specific charges. Our evaluation considers not just fee levels but also alignment of interests between platforms, managers, and investors through structure and incentives.

Minimum Investment Requirements (15%)

Accessibility for different investor segments is a key consideration. We evaluate minimum investment thresholds for both individual opportunities and platform access, with higher scores for platforms that provide quality access at lower minimums. We also consider whether platforms require accreditation and how they verify investor qualifications.

Liquidity Provisions and Investment Horizon (15%)

Private equity traditionally involves long-term illiquidity, but innovative structures can provide some liquidity options. We assess redemption features, secondary market access, expected investment horizons, and distribution mechanisms. Our evaluation recognizes that different liquidity profiles serve different investor needs and objectives.

Due Diligence Process and Transparency (10%)

The quality of platform due diligence on investments directly impacts risk management and outcomes. We evaluate the rigor of the selection process, transparency of methodology, and quality of information provided to investors. Platforms that provide comprehensive research materials and clearly communicate risks receive higher scores in this category.

User Experience and Platform Functionality (10%)

The investor experience throughout the investment lifecycle affects both satisfaction and decision quality. We assess the onboarding process, account management, reporting capabilities, and educational resources. Our evaluation recognizes that different interfaces serve different investor sophistication levels and engagement preferences.

Regulatory Compliance and Investor Protections (5%)

Adherence to securities regulations and investor protection mechanisms is essential for platform credibility. We evaluate regulatory status, compliance frameworks, and investor safeguards. Platforms demonstrating strong governance and transparent regulatory compliance score higher in this dimension.

Our evaluation process includes detailed analysis of platform offerings, investment structures, and legal documentation. We conduct interviews with platform representatives to understand selection methodologies and operational processes. Additionally, where possible, we gather feedback from current platform users to incorporate practical experience with investment process and ongoing management.

Scores are updated annually to reflect platform enhancements, track record development, and evolving market conditions. Our approach deliberately emphasizes access quality and economics while recognizing the importance of minimum requirements and liquidity provisions for different investor objectives.

Conclusion

The landscape of private equity access for individual investors has transformed dramatically, with innovative platforms breaking down barriers that once restricted these investments to institutions and the ultra-wealthy. Today's investors can access opportunities ranging from direct startup investments to institutional private equity funds with lower minimums, simplified processes, and in some cases, enhanced liquidity provisions.

When selecting a private equity platform, investors should carefully consider their specific objectives, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and investment horizon. Direct investment platforms like AngelList and EquityZen provide targeted exposure to individual companies at different stages, while fund access platforms like iCapital and Moonfare offer professionally-managed portfolios of private equity investments. Diversified solutions like Fundrise and Hamilton Lane provide broader private market exposure with varying degrees of liquidity and minimum requirements.

The democratization of private equity brings both opportunities and responsibilities for individual investors. While these platforms create access to potentially attractive investments previously unavailable to most investors, they also require careful consideration of illiquidity risk, fee layers, and the inherent dispersion of returns in private markets. The most successful private equity investors maintain realistic expectations about return timelines, diversify appropriately, and select platforms aligned with their specific financial objectives and constraints.

As private equity platforms continue to evolve, we anticipate further innovations in liquidity provisions, fee structures, and minimum threshold reductions. Regulatory developments may further expand access while enhancing investor protections. The most successful platforms will likely be those that balance quality access with intuitive user experience and transparent economics, creating sustainable value for both investors and the companies and funds they support.

For individual investors willing to accept the unique characteristics of private equity investing, these platforms offer unprecedented opportunities to diversify beyond public markets and access investment strategies previously reserved for institutions. By carefully selecting platforms aligned with their specific needs and maintaining appropriate portfolio allocations, individual investors can now incorporate private equity as a meaningful component of their long-term investment strategies.
