Alternative Investments

Professional platforms for private equity, real estate, hedge funds, and non-traditional assets

Alternative investment platforms provide specialized tools, data, and access to non-traditional asset classes beyond public stocks and bonds. This category examines digital resources for professional investors seeking exposure to private equity, real estate, hedge funds, commodities, and other alternative assets.

Our evaluations focus on access quality, data transparency, fee structures, and platform functionality. We assess each platform's suitability for different investor profiles from institutional allocators to high-net-worth individuals and wealth management professionals seeking alternative exposure.

Featured Review: Private Markets Investment Platforms

93/100
Overall Rating

Digital Access to Institutional-Quality Private Market Investments

Private markets investment platforms have transformed access to private equity, venture capital, and private credit investments for professional investors. This analysis evaluates leading platforms that provide institutional-quality private market opportunities with lower minimums and improved liquidity terms.

Our review compares iCapital Network, Moonfare, Titanbay, and Forge Global. The assessment examines investment quality, access requirements, fee structures, and platform functionality for wealth managers, family offices, and institutional investors seeking private market exposure.

Key Findings

  • iCapital Network offers the most comprehensive private markets platform with exceptional fund manager selection and due diligence processes
  • Moonfare provides superior user experience with the most transparent fee structure and performance reporting
  • Titanbay delivers the strongest integration with wealth management platforms and custodians
  • Total fee loads (platform fees plus underlying investment fees) remain substantial across all providers, though lower than traditional private equity access points
