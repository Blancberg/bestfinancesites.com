Overview: Technology Solutions for Retirement Security

Retirement planning and pension management technology has undergone significant transformation, evolving from basic record-keeping systems to sophisticated platforms that integrate financial planning, investment management, and administrative functions. These platforms help individuals plan for retirement security while enabling organizations to efficiently manage retirement programs with improved compliance, engagement, and outcomes.

This comprehensive comparison examines the leading retirement planning and pension management platforms, analyzing their capabilities across individual planning tools, organizational administration systems, and specialized functions. Whether you're a retirement plan sponsor, benefits administrator, financial advisor, or individual planner, this guide will help you identify the optimal technology solutions for your specific retirement management and planning needs.

Key Insights

  • Retirement planning technology has shifted toward holistic financial wellness, integrating retirement with broader financial planning needs and behavioral tools
  • AI-enhanced personalization enables more targeted guidance and engagement strategies that adapt to individual circumstances and behaviors
  • Decumulation planning tools have gained prominence as platforms address the complex challenge of retirement income generation and longevity risk management
  • Integration capabilities have become critical as retirement platforms increasingly connect with wealth management, benefits, payroll, and financial wellness systems

Comprehensive Retirement Analysis Packages

Beyond plan administration, a distinct category of comprehensive retirement analysis packages serves advisors and individual planners who need deep modeling of retirement readiness, income strategy, and longevity risk. These packages differ from basic retirement calculators in three ways: they model the full household balance sheet rather than a single account, they incorporate tax-aware withdrawal sequencing and Social Security claiming optimization, and they stress-test outcomes through Monte Carlo simulation rather than straight-line projections.

Among the platforms reviewed above, NewRetirement (now Boldin) offers the most complete self-directed analysis package, modeling more than 250 inputs including Roth conversions, annuity purchases, and home equity decisions. Income Strategy and Income Discovery lead on decumulation analytics — their withdrawal sequencing and claiming-strategy engines routinely identify five-to-six-figure lifetime improvements for retirees with multiple account types. For advisor-delivered analysis, the major financial planning platforms (eMoney, MoneyGuidePro, RightCapital) bundle comprehensive retirement analysis within broader wealth planning workflows.

What a complete package should include:

  • Household-level projection engine with Monte Carlo simulation and configurable market assumptions
  • Social Security claiming optimization across both spouses, including survivor and spousal benefit interactions
  • Tax-aware withdrawal sequencing across taxable, tax-deferred, and Roth accounts, with Roth conversion modeling
  • Healthcare and long-term care cost modeling, including Medicare premium (IRMAA) thresholds
  • Pension, annuity, and guaranteed-income integration alongside portfolio assets
  • Scenario comparison — retire earlier versus later, downsize versus stay, lump sum versus annuity

Industry Trends and Future Developments

Hyper-Personalized Retirement Pathways

Advanced retirement platforms are leveraging artificial intelligence and behavioral data to create individualized retirement journeys beyond basic age-based defaults. These systems analyze numerous data points to develop personalized savings rates, investment allocations, and engagement strategies tailored to individual circumstances, preferences, and behaviors. The approach significantly improves retirement outcomes by addressing the specific challenges and opportunities of each participant rather than relying on generalized advice.

Comprehensive Income Management Solutions

Retirement platforms are evolving from accumulation-focused systems to comprehensive income management solutions addressing the complex challenges of decumulation. These tools incorporate advanced longevity modeling, dynamic withdrawal strategies, and integrated product allocation across traditional investments, annuities, and insurance. The holistic approach helps retirees balance competing objectives of lifetime income security, flexibility for unexpected needs, and legacy planning.

Embedded Financial Wellbeing

Retirement technology increasingly integrates with broader financial wellness solutions addressing immediate financial challenges that impact long-term security. These platforms connect retirement planning with emergency savings, debt management, healthcare funding, and budgeting tools in unified experiences. The embedded approach recognizes that retirement outcomes depend on holistic financial health rather than isolated retirement account management.

Automated Pension Risk Management

Defined benefit pension administration systems are incorporating sophisticated risk management tools enabling dynamic liability-driven investment strategies and de-risking pathways. These capabilities help plan sponsors manage funding volatility, implement glide path strategies, and evaluate risk transfer options like annuitization through integrated modeling tools. The automation of complex risk management processes improves decision-making while reducing administrative burden.

Open Retirement Ecosystems

The retirement technology landscape is evolving toward open ecosystems with standardized data sharing and API-enabled integration between complementary platforms. This shift enables plan sponsors to create best-of-breed solutions combining specialized components rather than relying on single-vendor platforms with varying capabilities across functions. The ecosystem approach supports innovation while improving data portability and lifetime retirement planning across changing employment relationships.
