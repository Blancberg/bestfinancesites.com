Refinitiv Eikon Review

The leading Bloomberg alternative with modern interface and comprehensive global market coverage

88/100 Market Data Platform Last Updated: March 2, 2026

Refinitiv Eikon (now part of LSEG Data & Analytics following the London Stock Exchange Group acquisition) stands as the premier alternative to Bloomberg Terminal, offering comparable data coverage and analytics at 30-40% lower cost. With over 400,000 users globally, Eikon combines deep market data, powerful analytics, and a modern user interface that appeals to both traditional finance professionals and next-generation analysts.

Quick Verdict

Best For: Buy-side firms, wealth managers, corporate treasurers, and trading desks seeking comprehensive market data and analytics with better value than Bloomberg while maintaining professional-grade capabilities.

Skip If: You require Bloomberg's messaging network for sell-side communication, need the deepest fixed income corporate bond coverage, or work in environments where Bloomberg is mandatory for collaboration.

Overview

Refinitiv Eikon evolved from the Thomson Reuters legacy when Blackstone acquired the Financial & Risk division in 2018, forming Refinitiv. In 2021, the London Stock Exchange Group completed its acquisition of Refinitiv, integrating it into LSEG's data and analytics operations. Despite ownership changes, Eikon has maintained its position as Bloomberg's primary competitor through continuous innovation and aggressive pricing.

The platform's strength lies in its modern, web-based architecture that delivers institutional-grade data and analytics through an intuitive interface. Unlike Bloomberg's command-line approach, Eikon emphasizes visual workflows, customizable workspaces, and seamless integration with Microsoft Office. This makes it particularly attractive to asset managers, hedge funds, and wealth management firms that prioritize flexibility and user experience.

User Experience

Refinitiv Eikon delivers a significantly more modern user experience than Bloomberg Terminal. The platform features a Windows-native application that emphasizes visual design, customizable workspaces, and intuitive navigation. Users can create personalized dashboards with drag-and-drop widgets, making it easier to build tailored workflows without memorizing function codes.

Interface & Navigation

Eikon's ribbon-based interface will feel familiar to Microsoft Office users. The platform organizes features into logical categories (Markets, News, Analytics, Research) accessible via top-level navigation. The Search Everything function allows users to find instruments, companies, people, and functions through a single search bar. Workspace templates help new users get started quickly.

Customization & Flexibility

One of Eikon's standout features is workspace customization. Users can save unlimited workspace layouts, share them with colleagues, and switch between them based on tasks. The platform supports multiple monitors seamlessly and allows users to pop out any component into separate windows. Color schemes, chart styles, and data layouts can all be personalized.

Mobile & Remote Access

Eikon provides web-based access through any modern browser without additional software installation. The Refinitiv Messenger mobile app offers messaging and market monitoring on iOS and Android. However, the mobile experience focuses on monitoring and communication rather than deep analysis. Remote access is included in base pricing without additional fees.

Training & Support

Refinitiv offers comprehensive training through Refinitiv Training, featuring self-paced e-learning modules, live webinars, and on-site instruction. The platform includes built-in tutorials and contextual help. Support is available 24/7 via phone, chat, and email, though response times can vary. The user community forums provide peer-to-peer assistance.

Performance & Reliability

  • Uptime: 99.7% availability with planned maintenance windows
  • Data latency: Sub-second updates for liquid markets, slightly slower than Bloomberg
  • Processing speed: Good performance for most tasks, occasional lag on complex analytics
  • Data accuracy: Generally high quality, with occasional discrepancies in emerging markets
  • Infrastructure: Cloud-based architecture with global data centers

Eikon's performance is generally solid but can experience slowdowns during periods of extreme market volatility or when running computationally intensive analytics. The web-based architecture means performance can be affected by internet connection quality. Refinitiv has invested heavily in infrastructure following the LSEG acquisition, improving reliability and speed.

Comparison with Competitors

Eikon vs. Bloomberg Terminal

Eikon offers 80-85% of Bloomberg's functionality at 60-70% of the cost. While Eikon matches or exceeds Bloomberg in FX, commodities, and derivatives coverage, it falls short in fixed income (especially corporate bonds) and lacks Bloomberg's ubiquitous messaging network. Eikon's modern interface and Office integration appeal to younger analysts, while Bloomberg's command-line efficiency still wins with power users. For buy-side firms without heavy sell-side communication needs, Eikon delivers excellent value.

Eikon vs. FactSet

FactSet excels in equity fundamental data, private companies, and portfolio analytics, making it the preferred choice for equity-focused asset managers. Eikon provides superior real-time market data, trading functionality, and broader asset class coverage. Eikon is better for traders and multi-asset investors, while FactSet suits fundamental equity analysts. Pricing is comparable, with both significantly cheaper than Bloomberg.

Eikon vs. S&P Capital IQ

Capital IQ specializes in company financials, M&A data, and private equity information at a lower price point. Eikon offers vastly superior real-time market data, trading tools, and news coverage. Capital IQ is web-based and more accessible for occasional users, while Eikon requires professional commitment. Firms often use both: Capital IQ for fundamental research and Eikon for real-time markets.

Eikon vs. FactSet Workstation

Both platforms compete directly for buy-side customers. FactSet offers better fundamental data models and portfolio construction tools. Eikon provides superior real-time data, broader market coverage, and stronger trading capabilities. FactSet's web interface is more modern, but Eikon's Office integration is unmatched. The choice often comes down to firm focus: long-only equity investors prefer FactSet, while multi-strategy and macro traders favor Eikon.

Use Cases

Hedge Funds

Multi-strategy hedge funds leverage Eikon for real-time market monitoring, quantitative research via Starmine, and API-driven systematic strategies. The platform's derivatives analytics support options and futures trading strategies, while the FX platform facilitates currency overlay strategies.

Asset Management

Asset managers use Eikon for portfolio construction, risk monitoring, and performance attribution. The Excel integration enables analysts to build custom models while pulling live data. Eikon Messenger facilitates communication with broker dealers for trade execution. The research aggregation brings together sell-side research in one searchable platform.

Wealth Management

Private banks and RIAs value Eikon's comprehensive market coverage and news for client conversations. The platform's reporting tools help advisors create client presentations. Lower cost versus Bloomberg makes it economically viable for smaller wealth management teams. The web-based access allows advisors to check markets remotely.

Corporate Treasury

Corporate treasurers rely on Eikon for FX hedging, interest rate risk management, and commodity price monitoring. The FX trading platform enables direct execution of currency hedges. The economic calendar helps treasury teams anticipate central bank decisions affecting funding costs. Integration with treasury management systems streamlines workflows.

Trading Desks

FX and rates traders appreciate Eikon's deep currency and fixed income coverage. The platform's dealing functionality allows traders to request quotes and execute trades without leaving the terminal. Market depth data supports algorithmic trading strategies. The low-latency feeds via Real-Time SDK enable automated trading systems.

Refinitiv Eikon stands as the most compelling Bloomberg alternative for buy-side institutions, offering professional-grade data and analytics at significantly lower cost with a superior user experience. The platform's modern interface, exceptional Office integration, and comprehensive market coverage make it an excellent choice for asset managers, hedge funds, and trading desks that don't require Bloomberg's sell-side messaging dominance.

The platform particularly excels in foreign exchange and derivatives markets, where its data quality and analytics match or exceed Bloomberg. The Starmine quantitative research and robust API capabilities appeal to systematic investors and quants building proprietary models. The recent LSEG ownership provides financial stability and access to the exchange's data ecosystem.

However, firms heavily dependent on Bloomberg Messenger for sell-side communication or requiring the deepest corporate bond data should carefully evaluate whether Eikon meets their specific needs. The platform's occasional performance issues during volatile markets and less comprehensive fixed income coverage represent real limitations for certain users.

For organizations seeking to reduce market data costs without sacrificing professional capabilities, Eikon delivers exceptional value. The 30-40% cost savings versus Bloomberg can fund additional seats, enabling broader team access to institutional-grade tools. Combined with the platform's flexibility and modern design, Eikon represents the future of professional market data platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much cheaper is Eikon compared to Bloomberg?

Eikon typically costs 30-40% less than Bloomberg Terminal. Where Bloomberg charges $24,000/year, Eikon ranges from $14,400-21,600/year depending on package and volume discounts. The savings increase with multiple seats.

Can Eikon replace Bloomberg at my firm?

For many buy-side firms, yes. Eikon provides comparable data and analytics for most use cases. However, if your team relies heavily on Bloomberg Messenger for sell-side communication or requires deep corporate bond coverage, a complete replacement may be challenging. Many firms use a hybrid approach with both platforms.

Does Eikon require special hardware?

No. Eikon runs on standard Windows PCs or via web browser. There's no proprietary keyboard or dedicated terminal required, making deployment and remote access simpler than Bloomberg.

What's the minimum contract length?

Refinitiv typically requires 1-year contracts with monthly or annual billing. This is more flexible than Bloomberg's standard 2-year commitment. Volume discounts are available for multi-year agreements.

Is there a free trial available?

Refinitiv offers 30-day free trials for qualified professionals and institutions. Contact sales to arrange a trial with full platform access and training.

How is Eikon's FX coverage compared to Bloomberg?

Eikon's foreign exchange coverage matches or exceeds Bloomberg. The platform provides superior FX analytics, deeper liquidity data, and better trading execution tools for currencies. This makes Eikon particularly attractive for FX-focused traders and global macro investors.
