Best Robo-Advisor 2026: Annual Rankings

Betterment, Wealthfront, Fidelity Go, Schwab Intelligent Portfolios, Vanguard — the 2026 robo-advisor rankings

Robo-advisors have matured into a stable category: pricing has compressed, feature sets have converged, and the right choice often depends more on which broker ecosystem you already use than on platform-specific advantages. The interesting differentiation now happens at the edges — direct indexing, tax-loss harvesting quality, alternative-asset access, and cash management.

Our 2026 rankings score the major robo-advisors across fees, tax features, portfolio construction, account types, and the integration with the broader broker ecosystem. Updated annually in January with rolling refreshes.

Snapshot

Betterment
91/100

Most polished standalone robo-advisor. Strong tax-loss harvesting, broadest goal-based planning, premium tier adds human advisors.

Fee: 0.25% (Digital) / 0.65% (Premium)

Wealthfront
90/100

Direct indexing for taxable accounts above $100K. Strong cash account. Acquired by UBS in 2022, operating independently.

Fee: 0.25% all tiers

Fidelity Go
89/100

Free under $25K, 0.35% above. Strong if you already have a Fidelity relationship; portfolio uses Fidelity Flex funds (no expense ratios).

Fee: $0 under $25K, 0.35% above

Schwab Intelligent Portfolios
86/100

No advisory fee but holds significant cash allocation that drags returns. Best if cash drag is acceptable.

Fee: $0 (but cash drag)

Vanguard Digital Advisor
88/100

Pure index-fund portfolios at 0.20% all-in. The right answer for buy-and-hold index investors who want some automation.

Fee: 0.20% all-in

Key Findings

  • Direct indexing — once a Wealthfront / Schwab differentiator — is now available at most major robo-advisors above a $100K-$250K minimum.
  • Schwab Intelligent Portfolios' cash allocation drag remains the most consequential trade-off in the category; the "$0 fee" headline is misleading once cash holdings are accounted for.
  • Hybrid models (robo + on-demand human advisor) have largely won out over pure-robo for accounts above $100K; Betterment Premium and Vanguard Personal Advisor are the dominant choices.
  • If you already have a major broker (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard), their robo is almost certainly the right choice unless you specifically need Wealthfront or Betterment features.

Best Overall: Betterment

Betterment retains the overall lead on goal-based planning UX, tax-loss harvesting quality, and breadth of account types (IRAs, joint, trust, crypto, 401(k) rollover assistance). The Premium tier provides on-demand certified financial planners for accounts over $100K.

Best Low-Cost: Fidelity Go

Fidelity Go is free under $25K and uses zero-expense-ratio Fidelity Flex funds, making it the lowest all-in-cost robo for smaller accounts. Above $25K the 0.35% fee is competitive with the rest of the category.

Best for Tax Features: Wealthfront (Direct Indexing) or Betterment

Wealthfront's direct indexing for taxable accounts above $100K (and at higher tiers, even more aggressive variants) produces the most tax-efficient outcomes for tax-sensitive investors. Betterment's tax-loss harvesting on ETF portfolios is the cleanest implementation among the goal-based planners.

Best Hybrid: Vanguard Personal Advisor or Betterment Premium

For accounts $100K+, both Vanguard Personal Advisor (0.30% all-in) and Betterment Premium (0.65%) provide certified financial planner access alongside automated portfolio management. Vanguard wins on cost; Betterment wins on planning-tool UX.

Best for IRAs: Whichever Broker You Already Have

For IRAs specifically, the practical answer is almost always the robo from your existing broker — Fidelity Go for Fidelity, Schwab Intelligent Portfolios for Schwab, Vanguard Digital Advisor for Vanguard. The integration with other accounts at the same broker is worth more than the marginal feature differences.

Methodology

Robo-advisor rankings score five dimensions: total all-in cost (advisory + ETF expense ratios + cash drag), portfolio construction quality, tax-feature depth, account-type breadth, and ecosystem integration.
